# Graveyard Keeper

Graveyard Keeper turns technology research and quest rewards into Archipelago checks.
Research follows its normal point costs and prerequisites, while the preview shows the
randomized items awarded by each check. Receive recipes, blueprints, gathering abilities
and upgrades from your multiworld to unlock them in your own game.

This integration is under development. Use matching APWorld and GraveyardKeepelago
client versions, and install every DLC enabled in your seed. The client currently
supports Graveyard Keeper on Windows with BepInEx 5.x x64.

Ordinary self-researched technologies deliberately award three checks together.
Recipe-book technologies have one check each. The three quest technologies use their
existing quest rewards instead of separate research checks.

The goal is either restoring the portal on Witch Hill or reaching the graveyard rating
chosen in the seed. Optional traps spawn bats or green slimes near the player.

See the [setup guide](setup/en) to install the client and connect.
